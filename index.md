# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 23, 2026

## Tsurerukana? Fishing Japan

Tsurerukana? Fishing Japan ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") provides fishing condition forecasts, fish species recommendations, lure recommendations, and other fishing information for fishing locations in Japan, including lakes, coastal areas, and saltwater fishing locations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses and processes, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

## Information We Collect

The App is designed to minimize data collection.

We do not require users to provide or create an account, and we do not collect or request personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Home or mailing address
* Payment information
* Contacts
* Photos or media files

The App does not require user registration or account creation.

## Location Information

The App may access your device's location **only if you choose to use the location-based features of the App and grant the necessary permission**.

You can use the App without providing your device location. You may instead select from predefined fishing locations available in the App.

If you choose to use your current location:

* The App uses your device location to determine the fishing area or coordinates relevant to your current location.
* Location information is used to provide fishing forecasts, weather information, fishing condition scores, fish activity estimates, and recommendations relevant to that location.
* The App does not require you to continuously share your location.
* The App does not use your location to track your movements or create a location history.
* The App does not sell or use your location information for advertising or behavioral tracking.

Depending on how weather data is requested, location coordinates may be included in requests to third-party weather or environmental data providers. Those providers may process technical information, including the requested geographic coordinates and IP address, as part of providing their services. Such processing is governed by the privacy policies of the respective service providers.

If you do not grant location permission or choose not to use location-based features, you can continue to use predefined fishing locations available in the App.

## Weather and Environmental Data

The App obtains weather and environmental information from publicly available weather and environmental data providers through third-party APIs.

This information is used to generate fishing forecasts, fishing condition scores, fish activity estimates, species recommendations, lure recommendations, and other fishing-related information.

Depending on the fishing location selected by the user, the App may request information such as:

* Weather conditions
* Temperature
* Wind conditions
* Atmospheric pressure
* Precipitation
* Marine or coastal conditions, where available
* Other environmental conditions relevant to fishing

Weather and environmental API providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and, when applicable, geographic coordinates included in API requests. Such processing is governed by the privacy policies of the respective service providers.

## Fishing Forecast Calculations

The App generates fishing forecasts and recommendations using calculations and algorithms developed for the App.

These calculations may incorporate:

* Weather conditions
* Atmospheric pressure
* Wind conditions
* Temperature
* Seasonal factors
* Lunar and solunar information
* Fishing-location characteristics
* Freshwater and saltwater environmental conditions
* Other available environmental data

No account or personal information is required to perform these calculations.

## Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

To improve application stability and reliability, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash reporting service provided by Google.

When the App experiences an unexpected error or crash, Firebase Crashlytics may automatically collect technical diagnostic information, including:

* Device model
* Device operating system version
* App version
* Crash logs
* Stack traces
* General diagnostic information related to application failures

This information is used to identify, investigate, and resolve software issues and improve the performance and reliability of the App.

Crash reports are not used by us to personally identify users.

## Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use advertising services.

The App does not track users across third-party apps or websites.

The App does not use advertising identifiers for marketing or behavioral advertising purposes.

Firebase Crashlytics is used exclusively for crash reporting and diagnostics.

The App does not use location information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or tracking users' movements.

* Weather and environmental data providers

When you use location-based features, geographic coordinates may be sent to third-party weather or environmental data providers when required to retrieve location-specific weather or environmental information.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

The App does not maintain user accounts or sell user data to third parties.

When location-based features are used, geographic coordinates may be transmitted to third-party weather or environmental data providers as necessary to provide location-specific weather or environmental information.

Technical diagnostic information collected through Firebase Crashlytics is processed by Google for the purpose of providing crash reporting services.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## Data Retention

The App does not maintain user accounts and does not store personal information on our servers.

The App does not maintain a history of the user's device locations or movements.

Location information used for location-based features is used to provide the requested functionality and is not retained by us as a personal location history.

Crash reports collected through Firebase Crashlytics are retained according to Google's applicable retention policies.

Third-party weather and environmental data providers may retain information associated with API requests according to their own policies.
