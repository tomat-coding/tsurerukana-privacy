# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

## Tsurerukana? Fishing Japan

Tsurerukana? Fishing Japan ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") provides fishing condition forecasts, fish species recommendations, lure recommendations, a personal catch log, and other fishing information for fishing locations in Japan, including lakes, rivers, coastal areas, and saltwater fishing locations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses and processes, where it is stored, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

## Summary

* No account or registration is required.
* Your catch log, catch photos, saved spots, and settings are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
* Location coordinates are sent to our weather data provider only to retrieve forecasts for the location you choose.
* We use Firebase (Google) for crash reports, remote configuration, and issue reports you choose to send.
* The App contains no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require you to provide or create an account, and we do not request or collect:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Home or mailing address
* Payment information
* Contacts

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device so that its features work. We do not have access to this information and it is not uploaded to our servers.

* **Catch log:** catches you record, including species, date and time, length, weight, lure, fishing position, notes, the fishing spot and its coordinates, and the weather and environmental conditions at the time.
* **Catch photos:** photos you take with the camera or choose from your gallery and attach to a catch. The App saves its own copy in private app storage. The App only accesses photos you select; it does not scan or read your photo library.
* **Saved spots:** names and coordinates of fishing spots you save.
* **Forecast location:** the location most recently shown on the Forecast screen, which may be your current location, so the home screen widget can show its forecast.
* **Reminders:** feeding-window reminders you set up, which are scheduled as notifications on your device.
* **Preferences and cached data:** language and app settings, the widget's latest forecast, and recently downloaded weather data.

You can delete catches and saved spots in the App at any time. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes all of this information from your device.

### Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's data (such as your catch log, saved spots, and settings) in your Google backup and restore it on a new device. Catch photos are excluded from cloud backup; they may be moved when you transfer data directly from one device to another. Backup is controlled by your Android settings and handled by Google under its privacy policy.

## Location Information

The App may access your device's location **only if you choose to use the location-based features of the App and grant the necessary permission**.

You can use the App without providing your device location. You may instead select from predefined fishing locations or spots you save yourself.

If you choose to use your current location:

* The App uses your device location to determine the fishing area or coordinates relevant to your current location.
* Location information is used to provide fishing forecasts, weather information, fishing condition scores, fish activity estimates, and recommendations relevant to that location.
* The App does not access your location in the background.
* The App does not use your location to track your movements or create a location history. Only the most recent forecast location is kept on your device, as described above.
* The App does not sell or use your location information for advertising or behavioral tracking.

If you do not grant location permission or choose not to use location-based features, you can continue to use predefined fishing locations and saved spots.

## Weather and Environmental Data

The App obtains weather and environmental information from **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com), a third-party weather data provider.

To retrieve location-specific data, the App sends the geographic coordinates of the relevant location to Open-Meteo. This may be:

* a predefined fishing location,
* a spot you have saved,
* the spot of a catch you are logging, or
* your current location, if you use that feature.

The App may request information such as weather conditions, temperature, wind, atmospheric pressure, precipitation, and marine or coastal conditions where available.

**Home screen widget:** if you add the App's widget to your home screen, the App refreshes its forecast in the background approximately every two hours. Each refresh sends the coordinates of the location most recently shown on the Forecast screen to Open-Meteo. If that location was your current location, the coordinates saved at that time are used; the widget does not read your device's location in the background. Removing the widget stops these refreshes.

Open-Meteo may process technical information such as your IP address and the requested coordinates as part of providing its service, under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Maps

When you add or edit a saved spot, the App displays a map using tiles from **OpenFreeMap** (openfreemap.org), which uses OpenStreetMap data. To load the map, your device requests map tiles for the area you are viewing, which means OpenFreeMap receives your IP address and the map area requested. This processing is governed by OpenFreeMap's own policies.

## Fishing Forecast Calculations

The App generates fishing forecasts and recommendations on your device using calculations and algorithms developed for the App.

These calculations may incorporate weather conditions, atmospheric pressure, wind, temperature, seasonal factors, lunar and solunar information, fishing-location characteristics, freshwater and saltwater environmental conditions, and other available environmental data.

No account or personal information is required to perform these calculations.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders and grant notification permission, the App schedules notifications on your device for upcoming feeding windows. Reminders are scheduled locally and are not sent through our servers.

## Sharing

You can choose to share a forecast image. The App creates the image on your device and passes it to the app you select (for example, a messaging app). The image may include the location name, date, fishing score, and forecast details. Once shared, it is handled by the receiving app and its recipients.

## Issue Reports

If you choose to send an issue report from the App, we receive:

* the issue category you select,
* the description you write,
* the App version, and
* the time the report was sent.

Issue reports are stored using **Firebase Cloud Firestore** (Google) and are used only to investigate and fix problems with the App. Issue reports do not include your name, email address, or location, unless you type them into the description. Please do not include personal information in the description.

## Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

To improve application stability and reliability, the App uses **Firebase Crashlytics**, a crash reporting service provided by Google.

When the App experiences an unexpected error or crash, Firebase Crashlytics may automatically collect technical diagnostic information, including:

* Device model and operating system version
* App version
* Crash logs and stack traces
* A randomly generated installation identifier
* General diagnostic information related to application failures

This information is used to identify, investigate, and resolve software issues. We do not use crash reports to identify individual users.

## Remote Configuration

The App uses **Firebase Remote Config** (Google) to enable or adjust features without releasing an app update, for example to prompt you when an important update is available. To do this, the App periodically contacts Firebase, which may process technical information such as a randomly generated installation identifier, the App version, device language and country, and your IP address.

## Analytics and Tracking

* The App does not use advertising services.
* The App does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
* The App does not track users across third-party apps or websites.
* The App does not use advertising identifiers.
* The App does not use location information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or tracking users' movements.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information is shared with third parties only as described in this policy:

* coordinates sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve forecasts,
* map tile requests sent to OpenFreeMap,
* diagnostic, configuration, and issue report data processed by Google Firebase, and
* forecast images you choose to share with other apps.

## Data Retention

* **On your device:** your catch log, photos, saved spots, reminders, and settings are kept until you delete them or uninstall the App.
* **Issue reports:** deleted 12 months after they are received. You can ask us to delete a report sooner using the contact details below.
* **Crash reports:** retained according to Firebase Crashlytics' retention policies.
* **Third-party providers:** Open-Meteo, OpenFreeMap, and Google may retain request information according to their own policies.

We do not maintain a history of your device's locations or movements.
